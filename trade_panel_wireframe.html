Design decisions to confirm
1
Order type — LIMIT not MARKET.
You said "buy at market_price × 0.99" — that's a limit order placed below current price. It may not fill immediately if price moves away. Is that the intent (patient entry), or did you want market order with a slippage guard?
2
Shares calculation. Notional $100 ÷ limit price = fractional shares. Alpaca supports fractional shares, but only for market orders on some brokers. For limit orders it rounds down to nearest whole share — confirm which you prefer.
3
SELL button — what are you selling?
If you already hold the stock → sell those shares at limit +yy%.
If you don't hold it → short sell? Or block the button?
4
Order lifetime (TIF). Should the limit order be: GTC (good-till-cancelled), DAY (expires at close), or IOC (fill now or cancel)? Day orders are safer for this use case.
5
Paper or live trading? The wireframe shows "Paper" in the header. Should there be a toggle, or always paper while building?
6
Close position button. Should this close at market immediately, or place a limit order at the current bid/ask midpoint?
Price Formulas (as coded)
BUY limit = current_price × (1 − xx/100)
e.g. $28.44 × 0.99 = $28.16
SELL limit = current_price × (1 + yy/100)
e.g. $28.44 × 1.01 = $28.72
Shares = notional_USD / limit_price (floor for limit orders)
e.g. $100 / $28.16 = 3 shares (rounded down)
Open questions before build
Q1 Broker API key — do you have Alpaca Trading API keys (separate from data keys)? Paper trading uses paper.alpaca.markets endpoint.
Q2 Fractional shares? Limit orders require whole shares on most configs. Should $100 / $28.16 = 3 shares (not 3.55)?
Q3 Positions refresh rate — poll the Alpaca /positions endpoint every N seconds, or only on order events?
Q4 News dropdown — currently it's a toggle button in the chart controls. Should Trade panel replace News entirely or sit below it?
